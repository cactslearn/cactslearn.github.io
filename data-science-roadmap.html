Career Path FAQs

What is the average salary for a Junior Data Analyst in Pune?

+

Entry-level analyst salaries in Pune range from ₹3.5 LPA to ₹5.2 LPA. Candidates with strong SQL skills and Power BI dashboard portfolios are highly sought after.

How is Data Science different from Data Analytics?

+

Data Analytics focuses on analyzing historical data to answer business questions. Data Science includes analytics but also covers predictive machine learning modeling and data pipeline engineering.
