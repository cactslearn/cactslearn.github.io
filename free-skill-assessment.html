Free 1-to-1 Evaluation

Programming Skill & Logic Assessment

Unsure if software engineering matches your background or aptitude? Take a free, personalized 1-on-1 logical and coding logic assessment with our senior developers. We evaluate your core logic capabilities and provide honest, direct feedback.

What We Evaluate

  • Analytical Logic: Breaking down systems and mapping nested conditions.
  • Syntax Aptitude: Recognizing patterns, basic loops, and structures.
  • Problem Solving: Formulating pseudo-code algorithms for simple logic puzzles.

Request Your Assessment Slot

How the Assessment Works

A simple, transparent 1-on-1 screenshare process with a senior developer.

1️⃣

Solve Logic Puzzles

We share basic flow charts and condition sequences. No programming knowledge is required; we evaluate how you break down complex steps.

2️⃣

Write Pseudo-Code

Together with your developer-mentor, you write simple text instructions to solve a task. This maps your procedural thinking capacity.

3️⃣

Get Honest Feedback

Your mentor provides immediate, direct feedback on your logic structure, recommending the optimal track (Web, Data, Cloud) or advising on prerequisites.
