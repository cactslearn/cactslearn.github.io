How to Subscribe to CACTS RSS Feeds

RSS (Really Simple Syndication) allows you to subscribe to content channels and receive real-time notifications without clogging your inbox. Follow these simple steps:

1. Copy the Feed URL

Click the "Copy RSS URL" button for the channel you want (e.g. Master Feed or Jobs Feed).

2. Open Your Feed Reader

Open your RSS reader application such as Feedly, Inoreader, Apple News, NetNewsWire, or Mozilla Thunderbird.

3. Paste & Subscribe

Click "Add New Content" or "Follow Feed" inside your reader and paste the XML link. You will receive updates automatically!
