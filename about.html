Our History

Founded in Pune in 2012, the Centre of Advanced Computer Training and Studies (CACTS) was established with a singular mission: to eliminate the shortcomings of mass-production computer lectures.

In standard classrooms, students sit through pre-written slides with little opportunity to ask questions or dive into real-world code. Over the past decade, we have perfected a virtual 1-to-1 learning model. We connect students directly with industry developers who guide them through live screenshares, explaining every compiler error and architecture decision.

14+ Years

Of Code-First Software Mentorship

We measure our success not by classroom size, but by individual student breakthroughs. Our mentors are working developers who understand current industry best practices.

Our Learning Pedagogy

We believe that software engineering is an active craft, not a passive lecture.

🎯

Personalized Learning Paths

Every student has a different learning speed. Since our sessions are entirely 1-to-1, we adjust the timeline and curriculum dynamically to match your coding aptitude.

💻

Practical Hands-On Learning

You will not watch pre-recorded lectures. Every session involves writing active code on your IDE, debugging databases, and configuring remote staging servers in real-time.

🚀

Live Project Internship

We bridge the gap between training and employment. You will gain git repository access and work on actual software products, receiving code reviews from developer teams.
