Fees & Payment FAQs

Can I upgrade to Data Science later?

+

Yes. You can upgrade your training path to Data Science or AI/ML by paying the pro-rata fee difference.

Why are CACTS fees lower than traditional class centers in Pune?

+

Traditional institutes charge high overhead for physical classrooms, sales counselors, and batch coordinators. CACTS operates as a lean, developer-led mentorship lab. We pass these savings directly to you, delivering premium 1-to-1 training at less than half the market rate.

Are there any hidden material or staging server fees?

+

No. The stated tuition is 100% all-inclusive. This covers your personal developer screenshares, code reviews, and staging server deployments.
